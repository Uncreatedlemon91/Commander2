status: accepted

**Decision:** A player's character, roster, equipment and cash live in a
player-owned company save that they bring into any host's world. World
state, faction reputation and contracts stay local to the host world (⚑ pending
confirmation, see inbox).

**Why:** Co-op progress isn't stranded in whichever world it was earned in.
