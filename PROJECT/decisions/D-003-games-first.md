**Decision:** Milestones end in a playable proof. Projects carry `media/`
(screenshots and clips), an optional `play_url`, playtest items and performance budgets.
The dashboard surfaces these directly. Non-game projects still work and just
leave those fields empty.

**Why:** For games, "can I see it running?" is the real progress signal.
